### Assembly mistakes

- **Forcing the CPU into the socket.** Align the notch and triangle, then lower the retention mechanism gently. Bent pins can ruin the board.
- **Forgetting standoffs.** A motherboard screwed directly to the case can short circuit.
- **Using too much thermal paste.** A thin, even layer is enough; excess can spread onto the socket.
- **Not seating RAM fully.** The clips should click into place and the module should be level.
- **Skipping the PSU wattage check.** A underpowered PSU can cause random shutdowns or boot failures.
- **Connecting front-panel headers by guesswork.** Check the motherboard manual; the polarity of LEDs and switches matters.

### Installation mistakes

- **Erasing the wrong disk.** Confirm the target drive and back up data before partitioning.
- **Booting the installer in the wrong mode.** Match UEFI or legacy mode to the disk layout and firmware settings.
- **Installing the wrong architecture.** Choose 64-bit for modern systems with 4 GB or more RAM.
- **Skipping drivers.** An apparently working system may still have unknown devices or poor performance.
- **Installing unlicensed software.** Confirm the licence and the client's requirements.
- **Not applying updates.** Install OS, driver, and application updates as required.

### Testing and documentation mistakes

- **Declaring success after boot only.** Test ports, audio, network, and stability.
- **Skipping the stress test.** It is a specific performance criterion, not an optional extra.
- **Ignoring temperature and error logs.** Random shutdowns and throttling are signs of a problem.
- **Leaving the workspace messy.** 5S is assessed.
- **No test report.** Documentation must be completed and forwarded.

### Practical tips

- Narrate what you are doing and why. Assessors assess understanding, not just motion.
- Keep screws organised and count them before closing the case.
- Photograph cable routing before disassembly if you are unfamiliar with the unit.
- When a fault appears, stop and diagnose instead of randomly replacing parts.
- Ask for clarification if the job order is ambiguous; do not assume.
