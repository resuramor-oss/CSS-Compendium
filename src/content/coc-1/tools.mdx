### Tools

- Screwdriver set (Phillips and flathead)
- Pliers (assorted) and long-nose pliers
- Wire stripper with bolt cutter
- Crimping tool and LAN tester
- Anti-static wrist strap
- Digital multimeter
- Soldering iron and desoldering tool
- Allen wrench set
- Flashlight
- IC insertion/extraction tool, tweezers, and inspection mirror
- Protective eyewear and other PPE

### Equipment and testing devices

- Desktop PC or workstation
- Monitor, keyboard, and mouse
- Network switch/router (for connectivity checks and driver testing)
- Bootable USB drive or optical installer
- Stress-testing software and diagnostic utilities
- Device Manager or equivalent

### Materials

- Motherboard, CPU, and CPU cooler
- RAM modules
- Hard disk or SSD
- Power supply unit
- Video card and sound card, if required
- Optical disc drive, if required
- Cables and cords (SATA, power, front-panel, display)
- Thermal paste
- Screws, standoffs, and cable ties
- Operating system installer and licence
- Device drivers and application installers
- Anti-static bags and cleaning materials

> **Tip.** You do not need to memorise the entire list verbatim. You do need to identify the correct tool for a task and explain why it is used.
