| Element | Performance criteria |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Plan and prepare for maintenance and repair** | 1.1 Plan and prepare maintenance/diagnosis of faults in line with job requirements. 1.2 Obtain and check tools, equipment, and testing devices for correct operation and safety. 1.3 Obtain materials and check them against job requirements. 1.4 Follow OHS policies and procedures. 1.5 Check computer systems and networks against the job/service order or instructions and specifications. |
| **2. Maintain computer systems and networks** | 2.1 Use appropriate PPE in line with standard procedures. 2.2 Check normal functions in accordance with manufacturer instructions. 2.3 Perform scheduled/periodic maintenance in accordance with manufacturer requirements. 2.4 Make needed repairs/replacements in accordance with established procedures. 2.5 Respond to unplanned events or conditions in accordance with established procedures. |
| **3. Diagnose faults of computer systems and networks** | 3.1 Use appropriate PPE in line with standard procedures. 3.2 Diagnose faults or problems according to requirements and standard procedures. 3.3 Manage and implement contingency measures in accordance with established procedures. 3.4 Respond to unplanned events or conditions in accordance with established procedures. |
| **4. Rectify/correct defects in computer systems and networks** | 4.1 Use appropriate PPE in line with standard procedures. 4.2 Replace or correct defective components or parts without damage to the surrounding environment or services. 4.3 Make adjustments as necessary in accordance with established procedures. 4.4 Respond to unplanned events or conditions in accordance with established procedures. |
| **5. Inspect and test the computer systems and networks** | 5.1 Undertake final inspections to ensure testing conforms with the manufacturer instruction/manual. 5.2 Check/test systems and networks to ensure safe operation. 5.3 Follow OHS standards and 5S principles. 5.4 Clean and clear the work site of debris and leave it in a safe condition. 5.5 Dispose of excess components and materials based on WEEE directives and the 3Rs. 5.6 Prepare and complete reports according to company requirements. |

## Range of variables (selected)

- **Tools:** pliers, screwdrivers, special tools, soldering iron/gun, wrenches, utility knife/stripper, hot air soldering station, LAN tester, crimping tools.
- **Equipment and testing devices:** maintenance bench, power supply equipment, multimeter, calibrators, signal generator, oscilloscope, appropriate software.
- **Materials:** disks, connectors, adaptors, wires and cables, software, storage media.
- **Systems and networks:** servers, peripherals, desktop computers, network devices, cables and connections.
- **Maintenance:** software updates, hard disk maintenance, file backup/restoration.
- **Faults:** network connectivity, software issues, hardware issues.

## Assessment methods

The assessor must select two of: observation with oral questioning, interview, or portfolio.

Resources that must be provided include tools, computers and peripherals, test instruments, materials, PPE, and technical manuals.
