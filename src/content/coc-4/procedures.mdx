### A. Plan and prepare

1. Read the job/service order and confirm the reported fault and scope.
2. Check the unit against the order and record its condition.
3. Prepare tools, testing devices, materials, and PPE; check for safety and correct operation.
4. Identify risks and plan contingency measures.
5. Back up data and record the current configuration before major work.

### B. Maintain

1. Follow OHS procedures and use PPE.
2. Check normal functions against the manufacturer's instructions.
3. Perform scheduled maintenance: cleaning, updates, disk checks, and backups.
4. Make necessary repairs or replacements according to procedure.
5. Record the maintenance performed.

### C. Diagnose

1. Gather information from the user and the job order.
2. Reproduce the fault if safe.
3. Check power, cables, connections, and recent changes.
4. Use diagnostic utilities and logs.
5. Isolate the fault by testing one variable at a time.
6. Identify the root cause and confirm it before replacing parts.
7. Document the diagnosis.

### D. Rectify defects

1. Use appropriate PPE.
2. Replace or correct the defective component without damaging nearby parts or services.
3. Make any necessary adjustments according to procedure.
4. Handle and store removed parts safely.
5. Respond to unplanned events according to established procedures.

### E. Inspect, test, clean, and document

1. Perform a final inspection against the manufacturer's manual.
2. Test the repaired function and general operation.
3. Confirm safe operation and check for overheating or errors.
4. Clean and clear the work site; leave it in a safe condition.
5. Dispose of waste according to WEEE and the 3Rs.
6. Complete the service report and submit it.
