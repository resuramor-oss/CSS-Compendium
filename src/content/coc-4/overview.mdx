This core unit covers the knowledge, skills, and attitudes needed to maintain and service computer systems and networks. It includes planning maintenance, diagnosing faults, rectifying defects, inspecting and testing, and documenting the work.

A competent technician does not simply replace parts. They follow a safe, systematic process and leave evidence that the repair was tested.

## What you must demonstrate

- Plan and prepare maintenance or diagnosis according to the job requirements.
- Obtain and check tools, equipment, testing devices, and materials.
- Follow OHS and use appropriate PPE.
- Perform scheduled and corrective maintenance.
- Diagnose hardware, software, and network faults.
- Manage contingency measures and respond to unplanned events.
- Replace or correct defective components without damaging the surroundings.
- Inspect, test, clean up, dispose of waste properly, and complete reports.

## Key terms

- **Preventive maintenance** — scheduled work that reduces the chance of failure.
- **Corrective maintenance** — repair work after a fault is found.
- **Diagnosis** — identifying the cause of a problem from symptoms and evidence.
- **Restore point** — a saved system state used to roll back changes.
- **WEEE** — waste electrical and electronic equipment.
- **5S** — Sort, Set in order, Shine, Standardize, Sustain.
- **3Rs** — Reduce, Reuse, Recycle/Recover.
