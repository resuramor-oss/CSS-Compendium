### Tools

- Screwdriver set (assorted)
- Pliers (assorted)
- Long-nose pliers
- Wire stripper and utility knife
- Soldering iron or hot-air soldering station
- Desoldering tool
- Wrenches
- Crimping tool
- LAN cable tester
- Flashlight
- Anti-static wrist strap
- Cleaning brush, blower, and lint-free cloth

### Equipment and testing devices

- Maintenance bench
- Desktop or laptop under test
- Multimeter
- Power supply tester
- Cable tester
- Oscilloscope or signal generator (where required)
- Diagnostic software and OS recovery media
- External storage for backups
- UPS or isolated power source

### Materials

- Replacement components (RAM, storage, PSU, fans, cables)
- Thermal paste and cleaning solution
- Connectors, adaptors, and cables
- Anti-static bags
- Labels and cable ties
- Service forms and technical manuals
- PPE: goggles, mask, gloves, anti-static wrist strap

### Documentation

- Job/service order
- Diagnostic and test reports
- Parts replacement record
- Backup and restore record
- Maintenance schedule and checklist
- Service report and recommendation
