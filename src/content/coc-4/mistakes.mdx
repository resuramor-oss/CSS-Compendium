### Diagnosis mistakes

- **Replacing parts without diagnosis.** Test and isolate the fault first; random replacement wastes time and money.
- **Ignoring the obvious.** Check power, cables, and recent changes before deep troubleshooting.
- **Changing several things at once.** You lose track of what fixed or caused the problem.
- **Not reproducing the fault.** If you cannot reproduce it, document the conditions and monitor.
- **Skipping logs.** Event logs, POST codes, and beep patterns often point directly to the cause.
- **Assuming hardware when it is software.** A corrupted driver can look like a dead device.

### Maintenance mistakes

- **No backup before risky work.** Always protect data and record the configuration.
- **Skipping preventive maintenance.** Dust and outdated software cause avoidable failures.
- **Using too much thermal paste.** A thin, even layer is correct.
- **Spraying liquid cleaner into components.** Apply cleaner to a cloth, not directly to the hardware.
- **Ignoring fan noise or high temperatures.** These are early warnings.
- **Not testing backups.** A backup you cannot restore is not a backup.

### Safety mistakes

- **Working inside a powered unit.** Disconnect power and follow lockout/safety procedures.
- **Opening the PSU.** Capacitors can retain dangerous charge; replace the PSU rather than repairing it unless trained.
- **No ESD protection.** Static damage may not appear immediately.
- **Poor housekeeping.** Clutter causes accidents and lost parts.
- **Improper e-waste disposal.** Follow WEEE and the 3Rs.

### Documentation mistakes

- **No job order or service report.** The work cannot be verified or audited.
- **Not recording parts replaced and serial numbers.**
- **Not recording tests and results.** "It works now" is not enough.
- **No recommendation for the client.** Note recurring risks or needed upgrades.

### Practical tips

- Explain your diagnostic reasoning to the assessor.
- Keep a consistent troubleshooting sequence.
- Photograph connections before disassembly.
- Use a multimeter safely and within its rating.
- Clean and organize the bench before and after the job.
