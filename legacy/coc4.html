CSS NC II · COC 04

Maintain and Repair Computer Systems and Networks

Review material for this Certificate of Competency. Use the sections below to recall the standards, procedures, and tools you'll need for assessment — this is a review aid, not a lesson.

Competency Standards TESDA TR

ElementDescription
4.1Plan and prepare for maintenance and repair
Plan preventive maintenance schedules and prepare tools/materials based on the job requirements.
4.2Maintain and repair computer systems and networks
Perform preventive maintenance and troubleshoot/repair hardware, software, and network faults.
4.3Inspect and test configured computer systems and networks
Inspect repaired systems and conduct post-repair testing to confirm restored functionality.

Procedures

  1. Identify the reported problem and gather symptoms
  2. Plan the maintenance/repair approach and prepare tools
  3. Perform diagnostics to isolate the root cause
  4. Carry out the repair or preventive maintenance procedure
  5. Reassemble and power on the system
  6. Test functionality and document the resolution
